# SurfCraft tutorials

Step-by-step guides, each with a short narrated video attached to the
[releases](https://github.com/sseveur/surfcraft/releases).
New to SurfCraft? Watch **SurfCraft in 3 minutes** (`SurfCraft-overview.mp4`) first.

| # | Tutorial | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Your first board](01-first-board.md) | New board from a template at your size, volume advisor, selecting and dragging points, fine drag, typed values in feet and inches, smoothing, undo/redo, saving | Ready (text + video) |
| 02 | [Rocker and cross-sections](02-rocker-and-sections.md) | Deck and bottom rocker, nose and tail rocker, stepping through, adding, moving and removing sections, bottom contours (single and double concave, vee, channels), rails (presets, softness, tuck, tail-to-nose blend), undo | Ready (text + video) |
| 03 | [Fins and print templates](03-fins-and-print.md) | Fin presets (thruster, quad, 5 fins), FCS I, FCS II and Futures boxes, per-fin toe, cant and position, fins in the outline and in 3D, the outline template at 1:1 with the fin table, tiling over A4, the 100 mm check bar, PDF export | Ready (text + video) |
| 04 | Fit in blank and CNC | Choosing a foam blank, Auto fit, toolpaths and G-code | Coming |
| 05 | Design by numbers and from measurements | Typing a board's dimensions; copying a board measured with a tape | Coming |
| 06 | [Asymmetric boards](06-asymmetric.md) | A separate outline for each rail (heel and toe side): which rail is drawn where, per-rail widths, 3D, fins placed from each rail, back to symmetric, BoardCAD compatibility | Ready (text + video) |
| 07 | [Reporting a bug](07-reporting-a-bug.md) | Detailed logging, Help > Report a bug: log period and preview, what to include, GitHub issue or e-mail with a zip (nothing is sent by itself), the log folder | Ready (text + video) |

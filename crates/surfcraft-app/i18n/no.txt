Depth is measured with a straight edge across the run-out points. Keeping the rails cuts foam as when shaping a blank: a concave raises the stringer rocker and the volume drops. Keeping the stringer rocker moves the bottom near the rails instead. = Dybden måles med en rettholt over endepunktene. The rail edge stays where it is and foam is cut away: a concave raises the stringer rocker by its depth (thinner on the stringer), a vee and channels cut towards the rails.
